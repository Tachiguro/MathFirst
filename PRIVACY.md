# MathFirst Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 13, 2026  
**Application:** MathFirst (`com.tachiguro.mathfirst`)  
**Developer:** Tachiguro  

## Overview

MathFirst is an offline mental arithmetic practice application developed by Tachiguro. MathFirst is designed to protect learner privacy by operating locally on your device without user accounts, advertising, analytics, or remote data collection.

## Data Collection and Sharing

- **No Personal Data Collection:** MathFirst does not collect personal data or sensitive user data for the developer.
- **No Data Transmission:** MathFirst does not transmit user data to the developer.
- **No Third-Party Sharing or Sale:** MathFirst does not sell or share user data with third parties.
- **No Advertising, Analytics, or Tracking:** MathFirst does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking.
- **No Accounts or Cloud Sync:** MathFirst does not require user accounts or sign-in, and does not provide cloud synchronization.
- **No Network Permissions:** The Android release does not request Internet or network-state permissions.

## Local Data Storage

Learning progress, learner state, and application preferences are stored locally on the device. Routine cloud backup of MathFirst learner data is not part of the product behavior.

## Device-to-Device Transfer

On supported Android versions, Android system functionality may transfer the approved local learner database directly during an authorized device-to-device migration. That device-to-device transfer does not send the learner data to the MathFirst developer.

## Data Deletion

Users can remove locally stored MathFirst data through application reset functionality where available or by uninstalling MathFirst.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

Future versions of MathFirst that materially change data handling may require this privacy policy to be updated.
