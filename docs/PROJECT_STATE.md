## 3. Current Implemented Learning and Persistence Baseline

- The current runtime implements independent progression for Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, and Division. The dense foundation contains 121 Addition, 121 Subtraction, 169 Multiplication, and 156 Division facts (567 total), followed by structured open-ended bands; the curriculum has no finite total size or permanent level-10 ceiling. Terminal dense bands are Addition 125 (`ADD-P8-D0`), Subtraction 135 (`SUB-P8-D0`), Multiplication 32 (`MUL-P7-SCALED`), and Division 32 (`DIV-P7-SCALED`).
- Operation scheduling and role selection are deterministic, and selection uses bounded evidence windows with lazy fact materialization. The global Mixed Checkpoint and global introduction lockstep are not active runtime concepts.
- **Deterministic Bounded Operation Scheduling (MF-STAB-002 Slice 2)**: Enabled operations are scheduled using deterministic bounded operation permutation bags. Every enabled operation appears exactly once per bag. Scheduling is deterministic, restarts deterministically, is independent of the order in which operations were enabled, and guarantees bounded per-operation frequency. Same-operation adjacency may occur across bag boundaries. The scheduler operates without RNG and without persisted scheduler state. Disabled operations are excluded from practice turns and hidden from the progress HUD while retaining attempt history, `ItemLearningState`, FSRS state, and `OperationProgression`; re-enabled operations resume preserved state.
- **Selector Model & Independent Per-Operation Role Authority (MF-STAB-002, MF-STAB-003, MF-LEARN-005, [ADR-0008](decisions/ADR-0008-independent-per-operation-role-ordinals-and-practice-configuration-reconciliation.md))**:
  - **Protected New Acquisition (MF-LEARN-005)**: When `requestedRole == PracticeSelectionRole.New` and the scheduled operation has at least one eligible unmaterialized candidate in its active introduction frontier, remediation does not preempt that requested New opportunity, guaranteeing foundational acquisition coverage.
  - **Remediation Priority**: When `requestedRole != New` (or when `requestedRole == New` but the eligible New candidate pool is exhausted), eligible Remediation ($\ge 4$ distance from previous error/timeout with `NeedsRemediation == true`) overrides requested-role resolution for the scheduled operation.
  - Deterministic requested-role cycle per operation across a 10-attempt period derived strictly from that operation's authoritative accepted-attempt count ($\text{NextOperationAttemptOrdinal}(O) = \text{AcceptedAttemptCount}(O) + 1$):
    1. New
    2. Due
    3. New
    4. Maintenance
    5. Frontier
    6. New
    7. Due
    8. New
    9. Due
    10. Frontier
    (Partition for $i = (\text{ordinal} - 1) \bmod 10$: `New` at $\{0, 2, 5, 7\}$, `Due` at $\{1, 6, 8\}$, `Maintenance` at $\{3\}$, `Frontier` at $\{4, 9\}$).
  - Global `PracticePosition` remains authoritative for total attempt ordering, FSRS virtual time elapsed, `DuePracticePosition`, and persistence validation, while per-operation accepted-attempt count is authoritative **strictly** for that operation's role ordinal within the 10-slot cycle. Existing curriculum, progression, coverage, pace, fluency, and item-state authorities remain untouched.
  - Requested New slots may introduce unseen owned-frontier material.
  - Unseen Dense material no longer globally overrides requested Due, Maintenance, or Frontier turns; requested-role authority is restored during Dense acquisition.
  - **Non-New Fallback Chains and Terminal New Liveness Escape**:
    - Non-New requested roles (`Requested Due`, `Requested Maintenance`, `Requested Frontier`) preserve their normal materialized review fallback ordering:
      - `Requested Due`: `Due` $\to$ `Frontier` $\to$ `Maintenance` $\to$ `EarlyReview` $\to$ `New`
      - `Requested Maintenance`: `Maintenance` $\to$ `Frontier` $\to$ `Due` $\to$ `EarlyReview` $\to$ `New`
      - `Requested Frontier`: `Frontier` $\to$ `Due` $\to$ `Maintenance` $\to$ `EarlyReview` $\to$ `New`
    - The terminal `New` entry is strictly a liveness escape to prevent unserved practice positions and selector starvation deadlocks. It does NOT mean non-New turns freely introduce unseen material, does NOT bypass requested-role authority, and does NOT override eligible review or remediation candidates.
    - Terminal New may resolve the turn only after all legal materialized candidates in the scheduled operation's review pools have been evaluated and exhausted.
    - Terminal New remains strictly subject to:
      1. Curriculum ownership ($\text{owner}_O(F) \le B$, [ADR-0007](decisions/ADR-0007-curriculum-fact-eligibility-invariant-and-startup-resilience.md));
      2. Guided four-operation number-space gating (`GuidedNumberSpaceGate`, [ADR-0009](decisions/ADR-0009-guided-four-operation-number-space-gate.md));
      3. Deterministic candidate ranking domain authority;
      4. Universal hard immediate-predecessor exclusion ($\text{FactId}(t+1) \ne \text{FactId}(t)$, [ADR-0010](decisions/ADR-0010-evidence-adaptive-discovery-operation-specific-guided-decoupling-and-pace-calibration.md)).
    - The scheduled operation does not change.
    - Selection metadata truthfully records: `RequestedRole` remains the scheduled role (`Due`, `Maintenance`, or `Frontier`), while `ResolvedRole` becomes `PracticeSelectionRole.New`, with `IsMaterialized == false` and `IsNewIntroduction == true`.
    - If no legal materialized candidate AND no eligible New candidate exist for the scheduled operation, the selector fails closed by throwing `InvalidOperationException`.
  - Frontier is materialized active-band consolidation; Due and Maintenance provide retention and review opportunities.
  - `Early Review` remains a fallback-only liveness bridge within review chains before terminal New.
  - Ordinary New introductions remain bounded by the four requested New opportunities per ten per-operation attempts, plus evidence-adaptive discovery and terminal liveness resolution.
  - Practice selection incorporates deterministic fact candidate ranking, anti-ladder candidate selection when alternatives exist, preserved exact (3-fact) and commutative mirror (3-fact) cooldown semantics, and deterministic role-specific ranking domains.
  - **Same-Operation Diversity (MF-LEARN-005)**: Inside the selected semantic pool, strict candidate selection avoids immediately repeating the previous same-operation `FactId` when another viable `FactId` exists. Cooldown relaxation tiers still permit repetition when necessary for terminal liveness.

Exact live and cold-restart reproduction at StoreRevision 5 proved the defect was selector terminal-liveness starvation:

- **Schedule-Agnostic SQLite Persistence Contract & Durable Count Reconstruction**: `SqliteLearnerStore` persists submitted learner-state transitions and validates Practice Position monotonicity, attempt/progression consistency, canonical fact identity, result correctness, answer/outcome consistency, item and FSRS state consistency, canonical four-entry `OperationProgression`, attempted-operation mutation boundaries, band progression, revision conflicts, duplicate positions, transaction atomicity, and idempotency. - **Selection Evidence and Exactly-Once Recovery**: `PracticeSelectionEvidence` is scoped by `ArithmeticOperation` and `ProspectivePracticePosition`; evidence cannot cross either boundary. Cached evidence is optimization only and never scheduling authority. Required enabled scheduled-operation evidence is loaded asynchronously on demand for the exact operation and prospective position. Disabled-operation evidence may be prefetched best effort, and its failure cannot block enabled practice. There is no arbitrary wrong-operation fallback. If a durable submission succeeds but next-exercise evidence preparation fails, recovery prepares the required evidence without duplicating attempt history, Practice Position, item/FSRS changes, or store revision.
- **Authoritative Settings & Current-Fact Reconciliation (MF-STAB-003, [ADR-0008](decisions/ADR-0008-independent-per-operation-role-ordinals-and-practice-configuration-reconciliation.md))**: Settings changes trigger awaited reconciliation in `TrainingSession`. If the unsubmitted current fact remains enabled and eligible, it is retained with its exact identity, `FactInstanceRevision`, partial answer input, and remaining timer deadline preserved. If the unsubmitted current fact becomes invalid (operation disabled or fact ineligible), it is immediately discarded and replaced with a valid fact for the same prospective global `PracticePosition` with zero durable or transient learning mutations (no attempt, no timeout, no score mutation, no FSRS mutation, no progression mutation, no attempt-count increment). If an answer has already been accepted (in `CorrectFeedback`, `IncorrectFeedback`, `TimeoutFeedback`, `TeachingIntervention`, `SessionCheckIn`, or other post-accepted state), the accepted learner state is preserved intact and configuration changes apply to the preparation of the next exercise. Settings reconciliation is awaited before UI navigation (no fire-and-forget). The operation HUD displays only enabled operations. The developer Statistics / Diagnostics section was removed from Settings as UI cleanup only; learner data remains intact.
- **Practice Time, Active Thinking Time, and Interruption Safety (P1, P1b)**:
  - *Current Durable Practice Policy (P1)*: Normal practice no longer enforces deadlines across any Practice Time preference setting (`HasEnforcedDeadline = false`). Clock passage alone never terminates a question, advances `PracticePosition`, advances the fact, clears partial input, or generates a Timeout outcome. Submitted answers are graded strictly by mathematical correctness regardless of response latency.
  - *Active Thinking Time & Interruption Handling (P1b)*: Response latency ($\text{ResponseLatencyMs}$) represents accumulated active interaction time across lifecycle interruptions. Inactive interruption duration (manual pause, backgrounding, settings/dialog navigation) is excluded. Active timing is segmented across interruptions. An uninterrupted learner who thinks slowly remains genuinely slow without artificial cutoff.
  - *Interruption Fact & Timing Eligibility*: `AttemptRecord.IsInterrupted` records as a durable empirical boolean fact whether at least one lifecycle interruption occurred during active answering. Timing evidence eligibility is derived as `TimingEvidenceEligible = !IsInterrupted` (not separately persisted or exported). `IsFluent` represents active-latency classification against expected pace; an attempt can be both `IsFluent == true` and `IsInterrupted == true`.
  - *Item Learning State Updates*:
    - *Interrupted Correct*: Mathematical state updates normally (`TotalAttempts` increments, `CorrectAttempts` increments, `ConsecutiveCorrectStreak` increments, remediation clears). Timing/fluency state is neutral (`FluentStreak` preserved, `IsProvisionallyMastered` preserved, `LastLatencyMs` preserved, `RollingLatencyMs` preserved).
    - *Interrupted Incorrect*: Mathematical error semantics apply (`TotalAttempts` increments, `IncorrectAttempts` increments, streaks reset, provisional mastery revoked, remediation due), but contaminated latency is not sampled into `LastLatencyMs` or `RollingLatencyMs`.
  - *Adaptive Pace Shrinkage*: Interrupted Correct attempts are excluded from latency shrinkage at learner, operation, band, and fact levels. Interrupted Incorrect and historical Timeout continue contributing outcome/instability evidence.
  - *Presentation Context*: New normal-practice attempts record `PresentedDeadlineMs = null` in presentation context.
  - *Timer & UI Mechanics*: `PracticeCountdownTimer` remains generic for future timed modes, but normal practice UI (`Home.razor`) does not wire automatic timeout callbacks. Manual Pause, Settings navigation, and same-process background/suspend freeze elapsed active time. A cold restart begins the in-flight elapsed timer fresh while preserving learner progress.
  - *Settings Simplification & Plumbing Retention (P4)*: Normal Settings eliminates obsolete user-facing Practice Time selection (Standard, No Time Pressure, 30s, 45s, 60s). - **Authoritative Dense & Structured Band Progression (Dual Window)**:
  - *Dense Bands*: Advance based on complete owned-frontier coverage and latest-outcome correctness ($C \cdot 10 \ge N \cdot 9$, where $N$ is owned frontier size and $C$ is count of facts whose latest attempt is Correct) evaluated over `PracticePosition > BandStartedPracticePosition` with in-memory candidate overlay. Errors and timeouts are recoverable by subsequent correct attempts. Fast Acquisition and the `MUL-D01` special 12-attempt bootstrap are retired; `MUL-D01` advances under the universal Dense rule ($N=4 \implies 4$ Correct). Dense progression remains purely mathematical/correctness-driven and does not evaluate timing eligibility.
  - *Structured Bands (Dual Window Policy)*: Advancement evaluates the Dual Window policy retaining canonical gates (40 / 38 / 34 / 20 / 16):
    - **Window A**: Latest 40 qualifying mathematical attempts in the current band instance. Used for mathematical window sufficiency, Correct count ($\ge 38$), frontier attempts ($\ge 20$), distinct frontier coverage ($\ge \min(16, N)$), and representative sample introductions ($\ge 16$). Interrupted attempts remain in Window A.
    - **Window B**: Latest 40 timing-eligible qualifying attempts in the same band instance. Used strictly for fluency: requires a full 40 eligible attempts with at least 34 fluent (`IsFluent == true`). Interrupted attempts neither help nor hurt Window B. Uninterrupted slow Correct and uninterrupted Incorrect count as eligible non-fluent evidence. No proportional formula.
  - *Runtime History Expansion*: To support Dual-Window evaluation and restart equivalence, the runtime retains the union of latest 40 total positioned attempts and latest 40 timing-eligible positioned attempts per operation (deduplicated by `SubmissionId`, ordered deterministically by `PracticePosition`, theoretical bound up to 323 records).

A persistent random pseudonymous installation UUID correlates cross-session exports without identifying the user or device; Full Local Reset purges temporary share files and clears the persistent installation ID. Strict learning non-interference regression tests guarantee zero mutation to FSRS-6, item states, progression, pace calibration, the 482 benchmark, or Cyber Defense calibration.
- **Practice Fact Eligibility Invariant & Defense-in-Depth ([ADR-0007](decisions/ADR-0007-curriculum-fact-eligibility-invariant-and-startup-resilience.md))**:

- **Cyber Defense Digit-Scaled Critical Hit Timing & Shared Authority (P2b)**:
  - *Product Rule*: $\text{CriticalHitThresholdMs} = \text{CurrentFactEasyThresholdMs} \times \text{DigitCount}(\text{CurrentFact.CorrectResult})$.
  - *Boundary Scaling*: 1 digit ($0, 9$) $\to 1\times$; 2 digits ($10, 99$) $\to 2\times$; 3 digits ($100, 999$) $\to 3\times$; 4 digits ($1000$) $\to 4\times$.
  - *Shared Single Authority*: Both the Cyber Defense radar arc/countdown and the Critical Hit scoring decision consume `Session.CurrentFactCriticalHitThresholdMs`.
  - *Calibration Semantics*: Critical Hits remain available only after pace calibration readiness ($\ge 24$ timing-eligible positioned Correct attempts). Correct answers outside the Critical Hit window still deal normal 1 HP damage.
  - *Strict Learning Non-Interference Boundary*: Digit-scaled Critical Hit timing is strictly a presentation and gameplay threshold. It does NOT alter underlying adaptive learning Easy threshold, `ResponseLatencyMs`, `AttemptOutcome`, `IsFluent`, `IsInterrupted`, `TimingEvidenceEligible`, `CurrentFactEasyThresholdMs`, `CurrentFactFluencyThresholdMs`, `CurrentFactExpectedPaceMs`, `AdaptiveAttemptClassifier`, `FsrsRatingMapper`, FSRS card state, adaptive pace shrinkage, progression rules, remediation, `PracticePosition`, telemetry export, or SQLite persistence. - **Dedicated Gameplay Store & Idempotent Submission Consumer Baseline (MF-CYBER-003 Baseline / Merged PR #76)**:
  - *Dedicated Database Separation*: Gameplay persistence is isolated in dedicated SQLite database `mathfirst_gameplay.db`, completely independent of Learner Store (`mathfirst_learner.db`).

- **Guided Four-Operation Number-Space Gate (MF-LEARN-004, [ADR-0009](decisions/ADR-0009-guided-four-operation-number-space-gate.md))**:
  - **Guided Mode vs. Custom Mode**: Guided Mode is active **if and only if** exactly all four operations are enabled (`Addition`, `Subtraction`, `Multiplication`, `Division`). Custom Mode is active whenever any other valid non-empty subset is enabled.
  - **Addition Ceiling Authority & Multiplicative Presentation Eligibility**:
    - In Guided Mode, the maximum represented number across the complete unlocked canonical Addition curriculum prefix ($0..\text{BandIndex}_{\text{ADD}}$) defines the ceiling: $\max(F.\text{LeftOperand}, F.\text{RightOperand}, F.\text{CorrectResult})$ across all facts in bands $0..\text{BandIndex}_{\text{ADD}}$.
    - Multiplication presentation requires $\text{owner}_{\text{MUL}}(F) \le \text{BandIndex}_{\text{MUL}}$ AND $\text{CorrectResult} \le \text{AdditionCeiling}$.
    - Division presentation requires $\text{owner}_{\text{DIV}}(F) \le \text{BandIndex}_{\text{DIV}}$ AND $\text{LeftOperand} \le \text{AdditionCeiling}$ (since dividend is `LeftOperand`).
    - Addition and Subtraction are invariant under cross-operation gating (gate always allows).
    - Custom Mode operates completely unrestricted (`GuidedNumberSpaceGate.Unrestricted`), permitting focused single- or multi-operation practice without an Addition ceiling.
  - **State & Schema Preservation (`PERSISTED != CURRENTLY PRESENTABLE`)**:
    - The Guided gate restricts presentation eligibility only.
    - Progression `BandIndex`, `ItemLearningState`, attempt history, FSRS card states, fluency evidence, remediation queues, accepted attempt counts, and global `PracticePosition` are preserved losslessly in Schema V6 without migration.
    - Historical facts exceeding the ceiling remain dormant in SQLite and automatically regain presentation eligibility when the Addition ceiling expands or when switching to Custom Mode.
  - **Candidate-Window Anti-Poisoning & Multi-Layer Defense**:
    - Enforced defense-in-depth across SQLite candidate streaming (`ReadCandidateRowsAsync` excludes Guided-ineligible facts before candidate partitioning and window truncation, ensuring dormant higher-number facts do not consume any of the bounded 64 candidate window slots), snapshot evidence filtering, selector candidate pools (`New`, `Useful Frontier`, `Due`, `Maintenance`, `Early Review`, `Remediation`), and final selection boundary assertions.
  - **Evidence Cache Semantic Identity**:
    - In-memory selection evidence cache incorporates semantic gate identity (`GateIdentity(bool IsActive, int? AdditionCeiling)`). Equivalent semantic states compare by value equality of active status and ceiling. Addition and Subtraction remain gate-invariant (`GateIdentity(false, null)`). No gate identity is persisted.
  - **Settings Reconciliation Integration**:
    - Integrated with MF-STAB-003 `TrainingSession.ReconcilePracticeConfigurationAsync`: unsubmitted active questions that become Guided-ineligible are discarded and replaced at the same prospective `PracticePosition` with zero learning mutations. Valid questions preserve exact identity, partial input, and timer state. Accepted feedback is preserved until deliberate dismissal.
  - **Scheduler & Role Invariants**:
    - `DeterministicOperationScheduler` bounded permutation bag turn scheduling remains unchanged (25% nominal turn share per operation in all-four mode).
    - Per-operation role progression remains derived strictly from $\text{AcceptedAttemptCount}(O) + 1$ across the 10-slot cycle.

## 8. Durable Product Boundaries

- Core practice is offline-first and requires no account.
- Local in-app Privacy surface (`/privacy`) is readable offline without network access, analytics, or user accounts.
- Zero runtime networking or network permissions contract is preserved.
- Correctness and response latency are separate learning evidence.
- Negative subtraction, division with remainder, cloud synchronization/accounts, export/import, and larger-than-`Int32` arithmetic remain deferred.
- Active combat layout positional stability invariant: on-screen numeric keypad coordinates, arithmetic typography, and answer input area remain completely static across enemy spawn, replacement, boss appearance, HP/shield mutations, and floating feedback overlays; boss visual scale uses compositor-driven CSS transforms (`scale(1.42)`) decoupled from layout geometry (`visual scale != layout scale`); gameplay scroll suppression and defensive overscroll containment (`overscroll-behavior: none;`) are scoped strictly to `.training-host.active-gameplay` while Settings, dialogs, and non-gameplay pages retain normal scrolling (historical onboarding removed in P2) (`MF-UX-008`).
- **Core Learning-First Invariants ([docs/V1_PRE_STEP55_REFINEMENT_PLAN.md](V1_PRE_STEP55_REFINEMENT_PLAN.md), [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-cyber-defense-roguelite-gdd.md](superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-cyber-defense-roguelite-gdd.md))**:
  1. *Learning First*: MathFirst exists to automate arithmetic recall; game mechanics must never reduce learning quality.
  2. *Math Engine is Authoritative*: The mathematical domain core selects facts, evaluates correctness, updates FSRS-6 spaced repetition, manages band progression, and records learner history completely independently of combat state. Combat never alters arithmetic difficulty, fact selection, correctness grading, or learning progression.
  3. *Gameplay Must Not Control Learning Truth*: Game state has zero authority over curriculum, progression, FSRS, remediation, fact scheduling, or correctness.
  4. *Minimal Friction*: Normal use requires minimal non-mathematical interaction; interruptions and UI do not contaminate measured latency. Active thinking time strictly excludes gameplay presentation overhead.
  5. *No Loss of Learning Progress from Game Failure*: Game setbacks or encounter defeats never erase mathematical progress.
  6. *Skill-Based, Not Age-Based*: Progression adapts dynamically to demonstrated mathematical competence.
  7. *Learner Store Schema V9 Protected*: Learner persistence schema remains completely isolated from gameplay state.
  8. *Calm Mode Standalone Parity*: Calm Mode provides a complete, first-class, distraction-free arithmetic learning environment with 100% functional and mathematical parity with Cyber Defense.
